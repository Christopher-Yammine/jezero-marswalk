Jezero Marswalk Brief

Proposal: a crew-tablet EVA planner for Jezero crater that layers verified NASA orbital data, uses Perseverance's real traverse as ground truth, and shows a live astronaut position from a wearable tag we already have on the bench.

The pitch in one paragraph

An astronaut opens the tablet inside the habitat, taps a science target on a 25 cm HiRISE image of Jezero, and the planner draws a route that stays inside the 30-minute walk-back circle, avoids steep ground, shows how the thermal and mineral layers change along the way, and tells them what the surface will look like when they get there using Perseverance's own photos from that spot. During the walk the same map shows where the astronaut is, their heart rate and the fall alarm, relayed by a LoRa tag. The judges see a map that is honest about what is measured and what is modelled.

Why Jezero and not Gale or a blank site

  • Most data of any spot on Mars. NASA Trek serves a controlled 25 cm HiRISE mosaic, a 6 m CTX mosaic, a 1 m HiRISE DTM and a 20 m CTX DTM for Jezero only. No other site has that stack in one place.
  • Ground truth exists. Perseverance has driven 46 km over 559 drive segments since sol 14, and NASA publishes the full track with elevation at every vertex. We can prove our distance and slope numbers against a rover that actually drove them.
  • Human-scale science is on the map. The delta front, Séítah, Three Forks sample depot and the crater rim (Witch Hazel Hill, Bright Angel) are the exact kind of places a Marswalk would target, and they already have published mineralogy from CRISM and from the rover.
  • Weather and radiation have real numbers. MEDA on Perseverance gives Jezero temperature and dust; RAD on Curiosity gives the surface dose rate. We can cite both instead of inventing a "danger" colour.

Safety rules the planner should enforce

  • Walk-back limit. A 2026 NASA JSC paper on Mars suit mass proposes a 30-minute walk-back, about 1.8 km at 3.5 km/h. Draw that circle around the habitat or rover, and shrink it as consumables drop.
  • Slope. Apollo crews struggled above roughly 15 to 20 degrees with tools in hand. No published Mars suit number yet, so label ours "draft" and make it a slider.
  • Thermal. Jezero swings from roughly minus 80 C at night to around minus 20 C at midday in summer (MEDA; pull the exact sol series before quoting). Walk window = daylight hours only.
  • Radiation. Dose accumulates per minute outside; show a running mGy counter and a solar-event lockout.
  • Dust and visibility. MEDA optical depth; above a threshold, cancel the walk.
  • Buddy rule. Two astronauts, both tagged; alarm if separation exceeds a set distance.

Hardware we already have (from the Red Cross safety-tag bench)

Tag
ESP32-S3 with MPU-6500 fall detection, MAX30102 heart rate and SpO2, DHT11 temperature, NEO-6M GPS, E32 LoRa at 868 MHz
Network
Base and repeater boards, acknowledged compact packets, 1.4 s ping round trip, signal-lost after 50 to 95 s
Positioning
GPS outdoors, Wi-Fi FTM ranging to fixed anchors indoors at plus or minus 0.25 to 1.5 m, BLE search mode
Console
Browser console with a positions radar, vitals as green/amber/red, man-down flow verified end to end

Mars has no GPS, so the honest demo is: tag sends vitals, fall alarm and a position from the FTM anchors (habitat and rover as anchors), and the console draws it on the Jezero map. Rename the man-down flow to "suit emergency". Swap the heat-index rule for a cold-exposure rule. This gives the team a physical thing on the judges' table that no map-only team will have.

Suggested split for three people

  • Map and route engine. Leaflet with a Mars CRS over the Trek tiles above, click-to-plan waypoints, distance on the Mars radius, walk-back circle, elevation profile from the traverse data, slope flags.
  • Science and environment layers. Pull SWIM, MEDA, RAD numbers; place the science targets (delta front, Séítah, Three Forks depot, Bright Angel) with the CRISM reason for each; collect Mastcam-Z point-of-view images per target.
  • Crew tag and console. Port the LoRa tag to the EVA story, feed its position and vitals into the map, build the "suit emergency" alert and the buddy-separation alarm.
