EVA budget

Walk-back: 30 min at 3.5 km/h is the 1.8 km radius proposed in NASA JSC's 2026 Mars suit mass paper. No Mars suit slope figure is published, so the slope limit is ours. Daylight window taken as 07:00 to 17:00 LMST.

Each leg is checked every 30 m; the leg's slope is its steepest step, so a straight leg across a crater is flagged even if its ends are level.

Elevation comes from the USGS CTX stereo DTM (20 m, MOLA-referenced) resampled to 30 m, checked against Perseverance's track elevations where both exist (see Route comparison). If the DTM fails to load, slope falls back to the rover track and is unknown elsewhere. Dose uses 0.21 mGy/day, Curiosity RAD surface average at Gale, not Jezero (Hassler et al. 2014).

Terrain grid: loading THEMIS night IR…

Three routes from the habitat to the chosen target, computed on a 30 m grid inside the HiRISE mosaic. Shortest ignores terrain. Terrain-aware penalises ground that is dark in THEMIS night IR (low thermal inertia, a proxy for loose sand) and favours ground Perseverance drove. Recommended adds a preference for proven ground and refuses legs steeper than the slope limit where the rover measured elevation. Time is plain distance over walk speed; no terrain speed factor is invented. Click a row to adopt it.
